I craft intuitive, visually sharp digital experiences that actually work — no dark arts required. With 10 years of experience across B2C, B2B and internal tooling products, I've honed a deep craft in product design that spans everything from zero-to-one features to enterprise-scale design systems.

I was charmed into the design realm by my love for music and the creative culture it introduced me to as a teenager. My early start was in graphic design, but I became entranced by product design by accident soon afterwards. From there I polished my craft at start-ups and corporate companies within Chicago and Los Angeles. Interested in working with me? I'll even read your future with tarot if you're nice to me.
