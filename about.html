Hello, I'm Jennifer

✦ ✦ ✦

Los Angeles-based Senior Product Designer with 10 years of experience. Skilled in native iOS/Android, desktop products, and casting UX spells that actually work. Terribly skilled in sick music references and witchy things.

My passion for design was conjured out of being a lifelong melomaniac. I spent my formative years at rock shows, obsessing over merch graphics and tour posters until I was hexed into Photoshopping album artwork and Myspace graphics for local bands. Music and design felt like two entirely different forms of magic that somehow worked better together, kind of like Jay-Z and Linkin Park — doesn't make sense at first, but in the end it doesn't even matter.

I won't bore you with how I stumbled into product design. (Spoiler: it was less of a career plan and more of an accidental summoning.) For the last ten years I've toured the fintech, travel, healthcare, and industrial genres, designing for B2C and B2B and I'm incredibly well-versed at singing 'B2B' by Charli XCX in my car. I brew my best UX work while headbanging ideas into my unchecked ADHD brain with a metalcore album blasting in the background.

When I'm not conjuring pixel-perfect designs, I'm usually at some sort of concert or hanging out with my black cat Eloise. Every witch has to have her familiar. 🐈‍⬛🔮
