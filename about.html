Hello, I'm Jennifer

Los Angeles-based Senior Product Designer with 10 years of experience. Skilled in casting UX spells that actually work on native iOS/Android, responsive web and desktop products. Terribly skilled in sick music references and spooky things.

My passion for design was conjured out of being a lifelong melomaniac — obsessing over merch graphics and tour posters at rock shows until I was hexed into Photoshopping album artwork and Myspace graphics for local bands. Music and design felt like two entirely different forms of magic that worked better together, kind of like Jay-Z and Linkin Park. Doesn't make sense at first, but in the end it doesn't even matter.

I won't bore you with how I stumbled into product design. (Spoiler: it's an accidental summoning.) Ten years later I've toured the fintech, travel, healthcare, and industrial genres - designing for B2C and B2B, and at singing 'B2B' by Charli XCX in my car.

When I'm not conjuring pixel-perfect designs, I'm usually at a concert or annoying my black cat Eloise as seen pictured. Every witch has to have her familiar. 🐈‍⬛🔮
