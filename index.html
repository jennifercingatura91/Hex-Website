I craft intuitive, visually sharp digital experiences that actually work — no dark arts required. With 10 years of experience across B2C, B2B and internal tooling products, my knowledge is vast far and beyond. I was charmed into the design realm by my love for music and the creative culture it showed me as a teenager.

My early start was in graphic design but I became entranced by product design by accident soon afterwards. From there I polished my craft at start-ups and corporate companies within Chicago and Los Angeles. Interested in working with me? Contact me for more information. I'll even read your future with tarot if you're nice to me.
