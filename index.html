I craft intuitive, visually sharp digital experiences that actually work — no dark arts required. With 10 years of experience across B2C, B2B and internal tooling products, my knowledge is vast far and beyond.

My early start was in graphic design but I became entranced by product design by accident soon afterwards. From there I polished my craft at start-ups and corporate companies within Chicago and Los Angeles. Interested in working with me? Contact me for more information.
