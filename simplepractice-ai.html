SimplePractice

AI Chatbot & Home Page MVP

SimplePractice is a Santa Monica–based EHR startup serving mental health clinicians and their clients. As a temporary contractor for SimplePractice, I worked to enhance their B2B clinician app by developing MVP designs for an AI-integrated home page features and an in-app chatbot. These designs are currently slated for an initial iOS-exclusive pilot launch in Q2 2026.

Role

Senior Mobile Product Designer (Contract)

Duration

3 months (Jan 2026 – Mar 2026)

Responsibilities

Work with lead designer to understand feasibility, user problems and research on how to use AI in app. Speak with engineers about flow and any other questions that arose. Design UX/UI. Document flows within Figma for handoffs.

Before the magic

SimplePractice wanted to enhance the usage of AI in their clinician app and desktop product offerings. The goal was to bring in a chatbot and a new re-imagined home page to help organize and track clinicians’ workloads.

usability goals

Integrate a new home page to streamline clinician workflows, allowing users to review tasks, session notes, and client schedules at a glance. Introduce an AI chatbot that enables users to easily query information regarding client scheduling, daily insights, and general schedule management. Clinicians wanted personalization in the app tailored to them.

business goals

Streamline user productivity as clinicians are always on the go and rely heavily on phone and tablet options. With AI, it helps structure a clinician’s day to be more efficient and leverage the app’s capabilities.

Predicted outcome

Clinicians will feel empowered to organize their hectic schedules easier with the help of an assistant.

Brewing up the design process

This was a MVP design that was done pretty clear cut. I worked with my lead designer to review research documentation of pain points from clinicians for what they looked for in a home page and chatbot features. Additionally, I did some design inspiration research looking at other AI heavy apps and presented the ideas to the product team.


From there, I used Figma Make to populate some screens that showed potential of what things could be with the right requirements from product. After conversations with the product team, I took the original Figma Make ideas and spun my own take on ideas and applied SimplePractice branding to the designs. I also included iOS 26 patterns to the UI.

AI Chat Bot

I designed an AI assistant MVP to streamline clinical workflows, enabling users to organize tasks, track session notes, monitor business metrics, view chat history and manage schedules via natural language queries. I iterated on the bot’s placement within the global navigation to optimize accessibility while ensuring the architecture was scalable to support future feature expansions.

Wireframes

Home Page

For this MVP, it was strictly focused on showing a clinicians’ top three upcoming events, tasks of the day/week, view documentation and client notes. An action center was created to centralize these key items with the idea to customize the action center for post MVP.

Wireframes

Time to see if the magic holds

Light user testing was done with 2 clinicians to understand the direction of where the MVP was heading.

AGGGGGHHHHHH

One clinician likes the idea that they could see their schedule upfront and keep things minimal to the first three events of the day. It helped them look less “overwhelmed”.

And ACTION!

An action center was deemed worthy by both clinicians. Both said if it could expand later on that would be most beneficial to see other options we wanted to include but had to scale back from MVP.

Show me the money

Actionable insights about their freelance practice business or group practice’s overall business was key to them. They wanted to see monetary insights on revenue each week and month. While that option was scaled back from MVP, seeing concept work was welcome with praise.

The finished potion

While I did not get to see this launch due to the nature of my contract length, I helped push for a light version of what could expand on for the future. Here’s some hot takes of what is to come next after the MVP launches in Q2 of 2026.

Lookin’ Like Miss Cleo Seeing the Future

Some ideas for future full release was introducing higher level insights on the home page, more action center items like insurance claims.

It’s all about me, deal with it

Creating personalization within the app by acknowledging a name or birthday of the clinician seemed to be something that was a nice touch to add later on.

Getting Chatty

The chatbot was to expand to be immersive once SimplePractice could train their AI platform to do other intensive things that was suggested for the MVP. Once trained, the chatbot could evolve over time to do more complex tasks and basically be a BFF to a clinician.
