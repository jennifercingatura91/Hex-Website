Super.com

Flights MVP

Super.com, a B2C startup with travel, finance, and rewards products, aimed to launch a new flights MVP as part of its travel offerings to attract new customers. It was a fast-paced, high-pressure 0-to-1 product built quickly and under intense conditions. My sanity was lost somewhere along in the process. I was 5 seconds away from entering my Girl, Interrupted phase 🤪

Flights is under an NDA. This case study does not contain any mentions of in-depth metrics or strategy. I bet this is how Marvel actors feel about their NDAs when they can’t snitch about upcoming projects. Flights is available on Super.com and on Super.com’s app.

Role

Senior Product Designer (Contract)

Duration

5 months
(Feb 2025 – Jun 2025)

Responsibilities

Partnered with off-shore developers to understand feasibility and lend a guiding hand to new design direction, buddied up with a product manager and team leader on requirements, QA all design work, share out flow work to teams, used the best of my knowledge on UX functionality/UI best practices.

Before the magic

How can Super.com provide a new way to attract customers, retain their current customer base and gain new Super+ memberships all at the same time?

Usability Goals

Ensure users can easily search, book, and purchase a flight booking and ancillaries (checked bags) successfully. Showcase the best flight discounts online compared to other online travel agencies like Expedia and TripAdvisor.

Business Goals

Introduce a new travel product to compliment Super’s hotel product, aimed at increasing overall user engagement and loyalty. Focused on attracting new business by promoting Super+ membership benefits including travel discounts, cash back rewards and more.

Predicted Outcome

Creating a new travel product offering can help shape the Super.com business to gaining new customers to the Super brand and product, garner new memberships and retain current Super+ members. Lots of big risks with this being an MVP.

Brewing up the design process

Let me tell you something, this was a ‘no walk in the park’ project.
I was the last team member added to a high-pressure project and had just 2 months to design a fully functioning flight booking product—from the search homepage to post-booking flow. Yes, you read that correctly, 2 months.

I collaborated closely with the PM to clarify and question requirements, I used my prior airline industry knowledge to fill in gaps. I partnered with the hotel product designer to adapt successful UX/UI frameworks from the hotel flow to the flight flow. Before designing, I mapped out a UX flow chart to align the teams with. Then, I built the UX/UI screens, developed new UI components, leveraged the existing design system (Atlas), and partnered with a content designer on content all while continuously validating feasibility and strategy.

UX Flow Chart

Before starting design work, I mapped out the flight product flows, including scenarios like searching directly on Super.com and via a Google link. These maps covered all possible customer states during search, selection, and checkout.

Version 1 – Pre Rebrand

I designed responsive, high-fidelity Figma mockups for the end-to-end flight booking experience, including a new baggage feature. I iterated based on internal feedback and worked with third-party engineers to ensure feasibility before handing off via Dev Mode. Advanced features like seat selection were pushed to future phases, and I handled staging QA to catch bugs and ensure design accuracy.

I basically pulled a “Hail Mary” on this whole project.

Version 2 – After Rebrand

C’mon on Barbie let’s go party with the new brand colors! These new brand colors launched in July 2025 on the Super.com website and app. Before my departure, I proposed a new filter functionality for departure and arrival times that didn’t involve a clunky slider. This functionality essentially streamlines easy selection of flight times.

The finished potion

Super.com Flights launched in May 2025 to US customers on the website and app. Metrics were captured in Amplitude.

3, 2, 1, Launch!

Within 30 days of launch, flights had nearly 8,000 unique visits between app and website. There was a total of 121 confirmed flight bookings from current members and new customers.

Not much but honest work

There was a 12% attachment rate of new Super+ memberships subscribed during the flight booking process.

Make it lewk geood

After launch, minor design bugs appeared. I updated all UI bugs, QA’d developer bugs, and collaborated with a content designer to align with proper copy. I made suggestions to improve the sort & filter sections and investigated on how to solve for fare families feature.

It’s a Barbie summer

Super.com had a rebrand of their colors that I helped shape to apply to the flights feature. These UI updates took effect in July 2025.
